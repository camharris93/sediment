<details><summary>SQL behind this finding</summary>

```sql
SELECT is_churn_month, ROUND(AVG(active_users_avg), 2) AS avg_active_users, ROUND(AVG(mrr), 2) AS avg_mrr, COUNT(DISTINCT account_id) AS accounts FROM saasco_marts.mart_account_months WHERE month BETWEEN '2026-01-01' AND '2026-06-01' GROUP BY is_churn_month ORDER BY is_churn_month
```
