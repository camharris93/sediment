# ■ answer: Does having a salesperson involved help retention?

Sales-assisted accounts have a higher churn rate at 3.51% compared to 2.89% for non-sales-assisted accounts, suggesting that the sales intervention channel may correlate with elevated churn risk. However, sales-assisted accounts show slightly lower average tenure at 11.36 months versus 11.8 months for non-sales-assisted accounts, a modest difference of 0.44 months. The result spans 15,301 account-months for sales-assisted and 20,382 for non-sales-assisted, providing reasonable sample sizes; note that still-active accounts may understate their eventual tenure, and the churn rate reflects a monthly logo churn proxy rather than a discrete cohort measure.

| sales_assisted | total_account_months | churn_months | logo_churn_rate_proxy | avg_tenure_months |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| True | 15,301 | 537 | 0.0351 | 11.36 |
| False | 20,382 | 590 | 0.0289 | 11.8 |

```sql
WITH account_tenure AS (
    SELECT
        am.account_id,
        am.sales_assisted,
        MAX(am.tenure_month) AS max_tenure_month
    FROM saasco_marts.mart_account_months am
    GROUP BY am.account_id, am.sales_assisted
),
account_tenure_avg AS (
    SELECT
        at_inner.sales_assisted,
        AVG(at_inner.max_tenure_month) AS avg_tenure_months
    FROM account_tenure at_inner
    GROUP BY at_inner.sales_assisted
),
churn_rate AS (
    SELECT
        am2.sales_assisted,
        COUNT(*) AS total_account_months,
        SUM(CASE WHEN am2.is_churn_month THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS churn_months,
        CAST(SUM(CASE WHEN am2.is_churn_month THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS DOUBLE) / COUNT(*) AS logo_churn_rate_proxy
    FROM saasco_marts.mart_account_months am2
    GROUP BY am2.sales_assisted
)
SELECT
    cr.sales_assisted,
    cr.total_account_months,
    cr.churn_months,
    ROUND(cr.logo_churn_rate_proxy, 4) AS logo_churn_rate_proxy,
    ROUND(at_avg.avg_tenure_months, 2) AS avg_tenure_months
FROM churn_rate cr
JOIN account_tenure_avg at_avg ON cr.sales_assisted = at_avg.sales_assisted
ORDER BY cr.sales_assisted DESC
```
